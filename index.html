The outbound systemProspect to pipeline

Great services don't automatically create predictable growth.

Most agencies we speak to are good at the work. The delivery is strong, the clients are happy, the case studies are real. The problem sits earlier in the process: where the next ten conversations are supposed to come from.

01

Lead flow moves in waves

Two strong months, then a quiet quarter. Hiring, cash flow and delivery planning all become guesswork.

02

Referrals carry too much weight

Referrals are excellent business. They're also unpredictable, unscalable, and completely outside your control.

03

Generic outreach gets ignored

The same template sent to 3,000 people reads as noise. Prospects delete it in under a second, and your domain pays for it.

04

Prospecting eats real hours

Senior people spend afternoons in LinkedIn tabs and spreadsheets instead of pitching, positioning or delivering.

05

The data is unreliable

Bounced sends, wrong job titles, contacts who left two years ago. Bad data quietly ruins good messaging.

06

Nothing accumulates

There's no pipeline to look at on a Monday. Every month starts from zero instead of from a list of live conversations.

07

It all runs through the founder

When business development depends on one person's calendar and energy, growth stops the moment they get busy.

08

Effort isn't measurable

Without open, reply and opportunity numbers, you can't tell whether the offer, the list or the message is the problem.

None of this is a service quality problem. It's an acquisition system problem, and it's fixable.

How it works

Turn outbound into a client acquisition system.

Five steps, run in order, every month. Each one has an output the next one depends on — which is what makes the results repeatable instead of lucky.

01

Define

We work out who actually buys from you and why. Company type, size, role, and the buying signals worth acting on — a funding round, a new hire, a launch, a change in strategy. Everything downstream is built on this.

02

Target

We build the prospect list from scratch against that profile: the right decision-makers, verified contact data, and enough context on each account to say something relevant. No bought lists, no scraped dumps.

03

Engage

We write and launch outbound that reads like it was meant for that specific company. Angles are tested against real segments, and messaging is rewritten based on what gets replies — not on what sounded good in the first draft.

04

Qualify

Replies get handled and sorted. Genuine interest is separated from polite acknowledgement, timing objections are noted for follow-up, and high-intent prospects are prioritised.

05

Convert

Qualified opportunities are handed to your sales process with the full context of the conversation, so whoever takes the call isn't starting cold.

The challenge

A US-based PR and podcast booking firm needed a reliable way to reach new prospects at scale. Growth depended on outreach that could run continuously, produce measurable conversations, and stay relevant enough to get replies rather than complaints.

The targeting

We defined the profile worth pursuing and built a list of approximately 5,000 targeted prospects that matched it, with verified contact data. The list was the campaign's foundation — every number further down depends on getting this part right.

The outreach

Over 12,700 emails were sent across sequenced campaigns, with messaging written for the offer and the audience rather than adapted from a template. Deliverability was managed alongside the copy, because a 62% open rate is as much an infrastructure result as a subject-line result.

The engagement

The campaign reached a 62.0% open rate, a 17.8% click rate and an 8.0% reply rate. Replies were handled and sorted so genuine interest was separated from everything else instead of sitting in an inbox.

The opportunities

484 qualified opportunities came out of those conversations, representing $724,500+ in potential revenue based on the client's own deal values.

The result

485 bookings were generated in less than 3 months. The campaign moved the firm from ad-hoc outreach to a repeatable process with numbers attached to every stage of it.

These figures are from one real campaign for one client. Results depend on the offer, the market, the list and the sales process behind the outreach — so treat this as evidence that the approach works, not as a forecast for your own campaign.

FAQ

Questions agency owners ask first.

What types of agencies do you work with?

B2B service agencies of most kinds: PR, podcast guest booking and podcast PR, digital marketing, web development, SEO, branding and similar. The common factor is a defined offer, a clear ideal client, and a sales process that can handle conversations once they're created.

Do you provide the prospect data?

Yes. Lists are built and verified per campaign against the profile we agree, covering the right decision-makers with current contact details. If you already have data, we'll review and clean it before using it — bad data undermines good messaging faster than anything else.

Do you write the outreach campaigns?

Yes. Sequences are written for your offer and your audience, then rewritten based on what actually earns replies. You approve the messaging before anything goes out.

Do you manage the campaigns as well?

Yes — sending infrastructure, deliverability, launch, monitoring, follow-ups and reply handling. You can stay as close to the day-to-day as you want, but the running of it isn't your job.

How do you identify the right prospects?

We start with who has already bought from you and why, then define the profile from there: company type, size, role and the buying signals worth acting on. Prospects are then researched against that profile so the outreach has something specific to reference.

Do you guarantee meetings or clients?

No. Outcomes depend on your offer, your market, your pricing and your sales process — variables no outbound partner controls. What's committed to is the work and the transparency: targeting agreed up front, campaigns run properly, and honest reporting on sends, opens, replies and opportunities, including when the numbers are weak.

How long does it take to see results?

The first two to three weeks go into targeting, data, messaging and sending setup. Early replies typically follow once campaigns are live, and a clearer picture of what's working — angles, segments, timing — emerges over the following weeks as volume builds. Outbound rewards steady iteration more than a fast start.

Can you work with our existing CRM and sales process?

Yes. Opportunities are handed over in whatever way fits how you already work — your CRM, a shared pipeline view, or a straightforward handover with the conversation context attached. There's no requirement to change tools.
